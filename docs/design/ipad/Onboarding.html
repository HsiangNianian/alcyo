Onboarding

Three screens from install to a live board: say why the microphone is needed before iPadOS asks, pick where the board model runs, then start.

  1. Permission with a reason. The system prompt appears only after the screen says what listening is for and that speech stays on the iPad.
  2. One real decision. Remote (paste a DeepSeek key) or on this iPad (download in the background while you continue). Speech needs no setup.
  3. Placement over features. The last step teaches the two iPad things that matter: where to put it, and circling with Pencil.
