Settings · form sheet

Settings as a sheet

Settings opens over the meeting instead of replacing it, so you can change the model or the language without leaving the board.

  1. Two-column form sheet. Tabs on the left like the desktop, but 46 pt rows and grouped inset lists like iPadOS Settings. Swipe down or Done to close.
  2. Remote or on this iPad, as two cards. The choice that matters most is one tap; fields and model downloads sit under it, with a live "Connected" check and the key kept in Keychain.
  3. Speech is the microphone. No device-sound menu on iPad: Apple's recognizer by default, Alcyo's bundled models for mixed Chinese and English.
  4. iPad-only settings. Pencil double-tap and squeeze, finger marking, and keeping the screen on while listening.
