Meetings

On iPad the meeting list is a place of its own, not a strip beside the board. Boards are what people remember, so each meeting shows its board.

  1. Boards as thumbnails. A grid of real boards grouped by day, with who spoke and how long, reads faster than a list of titles.
  2. New meeting is the first card. One tap starts listening on the iPad microphone; the live meeting sits next to it with its timer.
  3. Search what was said. The search field looks inside transcripts and boards (⌘K with a keyboard); filters replace the desktop's Pinned group.
  4. Touch actions. Long-press a card for Pin, Rename, Export and Delete; drag a PDF from Files onto the grid to import it.
