Circle, then say it

The desktop's Circle tool, made for Pencil: draw a loop around anything on the board, then say what should happen to it.

  1. A loop selects. Lifting the Pencil closes the loop; whatever it touches is tinted in the accent blue, so you see exactly what was caught.
  2. Speech is the command. The card shows what it is hearing right now; the board model applies it to the circled items. Buttons cover the common cases silently.
  3. Pinch to work closer. The minimap appears only while zoomed in; double-tap with a finger fits the board again.
  4. Transcript as a pill. Collapsed, the card keeps the newest line in one row at the corner, out of the Pencil's way.
